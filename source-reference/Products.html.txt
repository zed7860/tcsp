Road KERB Stone
Road kerb stones, also known as curb stones, are essential components in road construction and urban landscaping. They serve several purposes, including:
Defining Road Edges: They help in clearly marking the boundaries of roads, ensuring vehicles stay within their lanes.
Drainage: Kerb stones assist in directing rainwater to drainage systems, preventing waterlogging on roads.
Aesthetic Appeal: They enhance the visual appeal of roads and pavements, providing a neat and finished look.
Safety: By providing a physical barrier, kerb stones help in preventing vehicles from veering off the road.
There are various types of kerb stones used in road construction, including:Low or Mountable Kerbs: Designed to allow vehicles to mount them easily.
Low-Speed Barrier Kerbs: Used in urban areas to prevent slow-speed vehicles from encroaching onto footpaths.
High-Speed Barrier Kerbs: Used in areas where high-speed traffic needs to be controlled.
Submerged Kerbs: Installed flush with the road surface for a seamless look
